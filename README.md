# Learning-with-Hsu-Learn-Japanese-
🇯🇵 Learning with Hsu – Learn Japanese! 💙

I've been working on my own Japanese vocabulary learning website, and I'm happy to share it with you!

The idea is simple: I want to make learning Japanese vocabulary easier, more organised, and more enjoyable. ✨

You can:

📚 ##Learn with Flashcards## – Study Japanese words and their meanings.

✏️ ##Take Quizzes## – Test your vocabulary and check what you remember.

🔖 ##Save Words to Practise## – Keep the words you find difficult and review them later.

📖 ##Explore the Vocabulary Library## – Browse Japanese vocabulary by category.

📊 ##Track Your Progress## – See how many words you already know, how many need more practice, and how many are still new.

Currently, the website has ##1,271##(N5+N4) vocabulary words!
